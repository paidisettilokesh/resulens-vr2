Our Solution

An All-in-One AI Career Intelligence Platform

ResuLens is a production-grade web platform that uses Large Language Models (LLMs) to deeply analyze a candidate's resume, provide actionable feedback, and generate career-ready content — all in seconds, completely free.

  • ✓Provide instant AI-driven ATS scoring and keyword matching for any job role
  • ✓Automatically rewrite resume bullets with impact-driven, recruiter-preferred language
  • ✓Generate tailored cover letters, LinkedIn rewrites, and JD-matched resume versions
  • ✓Deliver personalized interview coaching with AI-scored STAR method answers
  • ✓Reveal skill gaps and provide curated learning roadmaps with free course links
  • ✓Persist session history and support multi-format upload (PDF / DOCX)

Conclusion

What We Achieved & What's Next

  • Built a full-stack, production-ready AI career platform from scratch
  • Integrated dual LLM providers with automatic failover for reliability
  • Implemented 10 live AI modules covering every stage of job hunting
  • Deployed with persistent auth, MongoDB, and Google OAuth integration
  • Achieved consistent 1–3 second response times for all AI operations

⚠️ Known Limitations

  • Free-tier AI rate limits under high concurrent traffic
  • PDF parsing may lose structure for heavily styled resumes
  • Voice interview feature requires Chrome or Edge browser
